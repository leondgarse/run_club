Rules

  1. Hit pace, don't beat it. Faster intervals just ruin Thursday.
  2. Week 1 trial: sprint the last minute and note peak HR, then re-set HR zones.
  3. Back off if easy HR stays >155 at easy pace for a week, or anything aches >2 days: repeat the previous week.
  4. Strength 2×15 min/week: squats, lunges, calf raises, single-leg work.
  5. Heat: run early or late, and drink before & after.
